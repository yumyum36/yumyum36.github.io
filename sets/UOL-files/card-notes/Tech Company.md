Search for [Moxheart (UNB) cards here](https://yumyum36.github.io/search?search=tag%3Amox).



*Magic is a weapon; often a wild weapon. On the plane of Moxheart, however, magic has become not just a weapon, but an industry. A well-oiled, firmly organized industry. The various gemstones of Moxheart hold immense magical energy, and monopolies of gems fight a cutthroat war over their production and proliferation across the multiverse. Mass-scale wars have been fought by the draconic CEOs of Moxheart, with the most important goal; maximizing shareholder value.*



Underbrush Themes: Multi-color factions, fighting over gemstones/moxen, artifact theme, chromatic theme, industrialized magic, Treasures, capitalism


Tone: Mechanics first, flavor second, but a satire of capitalism, industrialism, and company culture.
