The [Season 13](https://yumyum36.github.io/search?search=tag:cc13) top 4 finals was to make 6 cards for one of the provided set briefs.

Search for [Underbrush (UNB) cards here](https://yumyum36.github.io/search?search=tag%3Aunb).



*If Bloomburrow is a plane of cute critters and cozy woodlands, Underbrush is a step smaller and a step less cute. A plane of insects, spiders, and all other sorts of itty-bitty beings, all fighting to survive in the great ecosystem of the Underbrush. And as the Great Winter arrives, the creatures of Underbrush must either work together, or work to win their game of survival.*



Underbrush Themes: Typal, but the types are all less-cared for creepy-crawlies. Swarms and hives and tokens to describe the littlest things. Food and foraging. Seasons? This should do for its demographic the same thing Bloomburrow did for cozy woodland fans.
